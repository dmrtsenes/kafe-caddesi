TITLE (24/30)
Cafe Avenue: Idle Tycoon

SHORT DESCRIPTION (77/80)
Run a coffee shop, serve customers and grow your team in a hands-on cafe game

FULL DESCRIPTION (1225/4000)
Cafe Avenue is a hands-on cafe management game with idle arcade flow and tycoon progression. Carry orders, collect your earnings and turn a small coffee shop into a growing business.

Prepare coffee and tea, bring toast and drinks to the right tables, and keep the takeaway shelves stocked. Follow customer orders and collect the money waiting at the counter. Touch and drag to move; picking up products and serving happen when you approach the right spot.

Keep your cafe ready for the next guest. Clear dishes, wipe tables and use your powered vacuum to clean the floor. Find your own rhythm between serving, preparing products and caring for the shop.

Build a team of shelf assistants, cashiers, bussers and baristas. Each role helps with specific tasks while you stay involved in table service and collecting earnings. Invest in equipment, carrying capacity and new areas.

Unlock more tables and rooms, then work towards a seaside branch. Choose decorations and uniforms to give your cafe its own character. Daily goals and festival tasks add new objectives as you grow.

This is an active business game: machines can build stock while you are away, but money is earned through active play.

Play in English or Turkish.
